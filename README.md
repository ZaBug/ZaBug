### Hi, I'm ZaBug 👋

I automate my home in Romania with Home Assistant: rooftop solar, an EV and a lot of sensors.
When an integration I needed didn't exist or stopped working, I wrote one.

**Home Assistant integrations** (all in the HACS default store)

- [EV Solar Manager](https://github.com/ZaBug/ev-solar-manager): charge your EV from solar surplus only, works with any charger that exposes a current entity
- [Duosida LAN](https://github.com/ZaBug/duosida-lan): local control of Duosida wallboxes, no cloud
- [Salus iT500](https://github.com/ZaBug/salus-it500): Salus iT500 thermostat with confirmed commands and data freshness
- [Energy Tree Card](https://github.com/ZaBug/energy-tree-card): household energy as a hierarchy, with kWh and cost

Feedback, bug reports and tests on other hardware are always welcome. Open an issue in the repo.
